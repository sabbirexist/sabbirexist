## About Me

<div align="center">

Hi, I'm **Sabbir** — a **developer** and **video editor** from *Shibchar, Madaripur*.

I design and ship **Telegram bots**, **streaming platforms** and **utility tools**, writing clean **Python** backends with **FastAPI**, persisting data in **MongoDB** and **Redis**, and deploying across **Railway**, **Vercel**, **Cloudflare** and **Netlify**.

On the creative side I cut and finish edits in **Premiere Pro** and **After Effects**.

I'm part of the **Kodex** community and contribute to open source, including the **WZGram** Pyrogram fork.

</div>
